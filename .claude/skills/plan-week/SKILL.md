Plan the athlete's next endurance training week.

Follow `CLAUDE.md` and the files under `training/`.

Before creating or modifying workouts:

1. Inspect the next 7-14 days in Intervals.icu.
2. Review approximately the previous 21-28 days of running, indoor cycling and outdoor cycling plus relevant fitness/load context.
3. Get current cycling FTP and useful running settings/threshold information from Intervals.icu when available.
4. Classify recent sessions by physiological intensity, regardless of sport.
5. Interpret any text supplied with this skill invocation as availability, duration or scheduling constraints.

Default to roughly three purposeful endurance sessions if feasible, but do not force three bike sessions when running or outdoor riding already supplies the intended load. Avoid consecutive hard days and keep no more than two hard endurance sessions per week by default.

If the invocation clearly asks to plan the week, create/update future workouts in Intervals.icu. Cycling sessions intended for MyWhoosh should be structured power-based workouts. Running sessions must use running-appropriate targets, not cycling FTP zones.

After writing, summarize the resulting week compactly and explain any important adaptation.
