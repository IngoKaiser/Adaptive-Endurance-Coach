Review today's training decision.

1. Read today's planned Intervals.icu workout and the surrounding week.
2. Inspect the most relevant recent running, indoor-cycling and outdoor-cycling activities and current load/wellness context.
3. Apply `training/coaching-rules.md`.
4. Choose: execute as planned, make a specific small adjustment, swap to easy endurance/recovery, or rest.
5. For bike workouts, provide %FTP and approximate watts when current FTP is known. For runs, use appropriate running targets and never reuse cycling FTP zones.

Do not alter the calendar unless the invocation explicitly asks you to change it. If it does, make only the changes needed and summarize them.
