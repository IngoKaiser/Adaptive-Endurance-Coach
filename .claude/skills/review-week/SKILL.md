name: review-week
description: Review the last multisport endurance week against the plan and identify progression, fatigue and next-step implications.
---

Review the most recently completed training week.

Use Intervals.icu to compare planned vs completed running, indoor-cycling and outdoor-cycling sessions. Evaluate:

- consistency and completion;
- total duration/load and hard/easy distribution across sports;
- quality-session execution;
- whether cycling FTP targets and running targets appeared appropriate;
- whether spontaneous outdoor rides/runs replaced a planned stimulus;
- whether the next week should progress, repeat, or reduce load.

Keep conclusions grounded in available data and user feedback. Do not diagnose health conditions. Do not modify future calendar entries unless the user explicitly asks you to apply the recommendations.
