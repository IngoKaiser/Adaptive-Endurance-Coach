name: reschedule
description: Move or replace planned running/cycling workouts in Intervals.icu while preserving recovery and the intended weekly training stimulus.
---

Reschedule the requested workout(s) in Intervals.icu.

Before writing:

1. Read the affected week and adjacent days.
2. Include recent running, indoor cycling and outdoor cycling when identifying hard vs easy days.
3. Avoid back-to-back hard sessions even when the sports differ.
4. Avoid catch-up stacking after a missed workout.
5. Preserve the intended weekly stimulus when possible; otherwise prioritize consistency and recovery.

Apply the requested calendar change, then report what moved/changed, the new date(s), and any meaningful training trade-off.
